# Code of Conduct

Ripper Evidence Modeler aims to be a useful and respectful open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to help keep discussions focused, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in issues, discussions, and pull requests.
- Critique ideas and wording, not people.
- Give actionable feedback when disagreeing with a proposal.
- Be careful with examples that involve resumes, employment history, companies, or private projects.
- Assume good intent, while still being clear about quality and safety concerns.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing tokens, internal URLs, customer names, or confidential project details.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainers through the repository's preferred private channel. If no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue that does not include sensitive information.
